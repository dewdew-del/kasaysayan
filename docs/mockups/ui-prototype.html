UX flow in this prototype

  1. Jump by era. Tap an era on the sticky bar. The bar highlights the era you are reading as you scroll.
  2. Filter by topic. Chips narrow both the list and the map pins. The real site saves this in the URL, like ?topic=war.
  3. List and map stay linked. Hover a card to highlight its pin, and hover a pin to highlight its card. Tap either one to open the event.
  4. Event page. Story, photo with credit, place, numbered sources, and previous/next buttons to read in order.
  5. Contested events get a yellow badge and a note that says where sources disagree.
  6. Nationwide events have no pin. They are listed under the map.
  7. Phones. Below 900px wide, a List / Map toggle replaces the side-by-side layout.
  8. Credits and fact-check rules have their own pages, generated from the event files.
